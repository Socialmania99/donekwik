Terms, privacy and takedown

DoneKwik converters and editors run in your browser. Your files are not uploaded to this site.

This is a static site. It has no server of its own and stores nothing about you except your own settings (favorites, theme) in your browser. The hosting provider may keep ordinary visit logs under its own policy.

Open-license search runs from your browser and contacts Openverse, Wikimedia Commons, the Internet Archive and Project Gutenberg directly. If you connect your own DoneKwik server, downloads go through that server.

You are responsible for having the right to any file you download or convert. Rights holders can send takedown notices to takedown@example.com.
